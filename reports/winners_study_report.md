## Reading the results honestly

1. **Survivorship.** Every token here was listed on CoinGecko.  A launch-time attribute that separates 1000x winners from listed controls is a *necessary-but-not-sufficient* signal: it does not tell you the odds among the ~700k launches that were never listed.  The RED-PUMP-2026 launch population is the fix.
2. **What the launch itself can carry.** Socials at creation, a real description, a non-serial creator and a fast king-of-the-hill are the only things knowable in the first hour; the classifier's AUC above is the ceiling of what they explain.  The remainder -- narrative timing, KOL/CEX attention, the macro SOL regime -- is exogenous and arrives *after* the launch window.
3. **Exits.** The drawdown block is the part that transfers directly to the bot: if most 1000x paths contained a >= 50 % drawdown before the ATH, no trailing stop of 30-50 % can hold a 1000x, and only a cost-recovered moonbag (or a ladder) keeps any exposure to that tail.
